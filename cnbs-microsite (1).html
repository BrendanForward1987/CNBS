About the Summit

Connecting Youth, Entrepreneurs
& Leaders for Eeyou Prosperity

Held in Mistissini from August 19–21, the 2nd Cree Nation Business Summit brought together youth, entrepreneurs, and leaders to connect, learn, and build a strong future for the Eeyou economy.

Youth Case Study Competition

Young Eeyou minds stepped up to the challenge in the Bear Den — pitching innovative business ideas and demonstrating the entrepreneurial spirit that will drive our economy forward.

2025 Theme

Eeyou Entrepreneurship in Focus

With a sharp focus on homegrown talent and innovation, this year's summit spotlighted the ingenuity and resilience of Eeyou entrepreneurs from across Eeyou Istchee.

Business & Leader Exchange Days

Two full days of workshops, presentations, panels, and roundtables fostering meaningful dialogue between established leaders and emerging entrepreneurs.

First Regional Awards Gala

The Summit's inaugural Awards Gala celebrated outstanding contributions to the Eeyou economy, recognizing the individuals and businesses shaping our collective future.
